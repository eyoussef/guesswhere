Photo geography game with open imagery

Guess Where! shows photographs from Wikimedia Commons, the free media
repository, and challenges you to guess where each one was taken. Five
rounds, up to 5000 points each: tap the world map to drop your pin, and
your score decays the farther you are from the real location.

- Photo packs: Whole world (random), Coasts and oceans, Mountains, Cities
- Daily Mix: day-seeded rounds with a stable mode each day
- Post-guess debrief: photographer credit, license, and file description
- Difficulty settings change how fast the score decays with distance
- Local leaderboard, personal-best tracking, and score sharing
- Fully offline-friendly map tiles (OpenStreetMap data via osmdroid)

No accounts, no tracking, no ads. Scores and seen-image memory stay on
the device. Not affiliated with the Wikimedia Foundation. Photos are
shown under their own free licenses with visible credit; map data is
© OpenStreetMap contributors.
